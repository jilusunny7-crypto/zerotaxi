1 Place Paper Against Wall

Place a blank A4 sheet flat on the floor with the short edge flush against a wall. Stand on the paper with your heel lightly pressing against the wall.

2 Mark Longest Toe

Hold a pencil vertically (straight up and down) and mark the paper at the furthest tip of your longest toe (either your big toe or second toe).

3 Measure in Centimeters (CM)

Measure the distance from the edge of the paper to your mark using a ruler. Match your measurement to the CM column in the table above to find your perfect UK size!
